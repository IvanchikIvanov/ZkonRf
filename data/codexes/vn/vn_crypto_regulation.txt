Section 1. Core legal acts.
Vietnam has moved from a gray-zone approach toward a formal digital asset framework. Law No. 71/2025/QH15 on Digital Technology Industry recognizes digital assets, including virtual assets and crypto assets, with effect from 1 January 2026. Government Resolution No. 05/2025/NQ-CP establishes a pilot market for crypto assets in Vietnam.

Section 2. Digital asset categories.
The Vietnamese framework distinguishes virtual assets and crypto assets from securities, digital forms of legal tender, and other financial assets regulated elsewhere. Crypto asset questions should be separated from payment-money questions because crypto assets are not the same as legal tender.

Section 3. Pilot market and licensed services.
Resolution No. 05/2025/NQ-CP concerns the pilot implementation of a crypto-asset market, including issuance, trading-market organization, service providers, state management, AML controls, and enforcement. A practical answer should check whether a platform participates in the pilot or is operating outside the permitted framework.

Section 4. AML and user identification.
Vietnam's pilot framework emphasizes customer identification, transaction monitoring, suspicious transaction reporting, cyber safety, and anti-money-laundering obligations. A user question about blocked accounts or exchange onboarding should collect KYC, source-of-funds, transaction-value, and platform status facts.

Section 5. Consumer and fraud issues.
Before the pilot regime and during transition, many user disputes may involve unlicensed offshore platforms, fraud, multi-level marketing, wallet theft, or payment disputes. Combine this source with Vietnam criminal, civil, cybersecurity, personal data, consumer, and tax materials.

Section 6. Bot checklist.
Ask: transaction date, whether before or after 1 January 2026, platform name, Vietnam presence, user residence, asset type, wallet addresses, transaction hashes, KYC records, payment method, suspicious-promotion evidence, and whether the user needs complaint, police report, tax, or civil recovery guidance.

Section 7. Legal tender boundary.
The State Bank of Vietnam describes Vietnamese dong banknotes and coins issued by the State Bank as legal tender and the unlimited means of payment in Vietnam. Crypto asset services and digital assets should therefore be separated from payment instruments and legal tender questions.

Section 8. Crypto asset services.
Law No. 71/2025/QH15 requires future government regulation of business conditions for crypto asset services, rights and obligations of parties, cyber safety, AML, terrorism-financing and proliferation-financing controls, inspection, and handling of violations.

Section 9. Pilot market risk.
Resolution No. 05/2025/NQ-CP is a pilot market instrument, not a blanket legalization of every offshore crypto platform. Answers should ask whether the provider is within the pilot, whether Vietnamese residents were targeted, and whether the transaction is issuance, trading, custody, brokerage, payment, or fraud.
