Special conditions for issuance of permits 
When issuing and extending a permit under an intra-corporate transfer, one of the following special conditions is imposed to an employer upon his/her consent:
Professional training of the employees who are citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan in the specialty of the foreign employee being engaged.
Re-training of the employees who are citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan in the specialty corresponding to that of the foreign employee being engaged.
Advanced training of the employees who are citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
Creation of additional jobs for citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan in the professions for which foreign employees are being employed.
Refusal to issue and extend permits 
A permit to attract foreign labour is not issued or extended in the following cases:
1) establishment of the inaccuracy of documents submitted by the applicant for the receipt of a public service and/or of the data (information) contained therein;
2) non-compliance of the applicant and/or the submitted materials, objects, data and information required for the provision of the public service with the requirements established by the regulatory legal acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
3) the existence of a court decision (judgment) that has entered into legal force prohibiting the applicant from carrying out activities or certain types of activities requiring the receipt of the relevant public service;
4) the existence of a court decision that has entered into legal force pursuant to which the applicant has been deprived of a special right related to obtaining the public service;
5) absence of the applicant’s consent, provided in accordance with

working in legal entities that are participants of special economic zones, with projects worth more than one million monthly calculation indices, as well as in organizations engaged by these participants of special economic zones (or their contractors) as a general contractor, contractor, subcontractor or service provider during the period of construction and installation works on the territory of special economic zones and before the expiration of one year after the commissioning of the facility (facilities) in accordance with the list of categories and numbers determined by the commission of representatives of the authorized body for employment of the population, interested central government agencies in the manner approved by a joint decision of an authorized body for employment and the central executive body carrying out state regulation in the field of creation, functioning and abolition of special economic zones;
those who arrived for self-employment in professions in demand in priority sectors of the economy (types of economic activity);
permanently residing in the Republic of Kazakhstan;
working as the first heads of branches or representative offices of foreign legal entities;

working in the Astana Hub International Technology Park or for participants of the Astana Hub International Technology Park as managers and specialists with higher education.

Fines for violations

Section 6. Original section/article: 519. Source: Procedure for employment of foreign labour in Kazakhstan.
of the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan as of July 5, 2014 "On Administrative Violations" provides for fines for the following violations in the field of foreign labour employment:
An employer's involvement of foreign labour without the permission of a local executive body or the use of the labour of foreigners and stateless persons who do not have certificates of compliance with qualifications for self-employment issued by an authorized body on migration issues or permits for labour immigrants issued by the internal affairs bodies, – entail a fine for individuals in the amount of thirty, for officials – in the amount of fifty, for small businesses or non–profit organizations – in the amount of one hundred, for medium–sized businesses - in the amount of two hundred, for large businesses - in the amount of seven hundred calculation indicators.
Recruitment by an employer of a foreign employee to a position (profession or specialty) that does not correspond to the position (profession or specialty) specified in the permit of a local executive body to attract foreign labour, – entail a fine for individuals in the amount of thirty, for officials – in the amount of fifty, for small businesses or non–profit organizations – in the amount of one hundred, for medium–sized businesses - in the amount of two hundred, for large businesses - in the amount of seven hundred calculation indicators.
The actions provided for in parts one and two of this article, committed repeatedly within a year after the imposition of an administrative penalty, entail a fine on individuals in the amount of fifty, on officials – in the amount of one hundred, on small businesses or non–profit organizations – in the amount of two hundred, on medium–sized businesses - in the amount of three hundred, on subjects of large business – in the amount of one thousand monthly calculation indicators.
The involvement by an employer-individual of labour immigrants in the execution of work (delivery of services) in the household without a corresponding permit issued by the internal affairs bodies, or conclusion of employment contracts for execution of work (delivery of services) in the household by one individual employer simultaneously with more than five labor immigrants – entails a fine in the amount of thirty monthly calculation indicators.
The actions provided for in part five of this article, committed repeatedly within a year after the imposition of an administrative penalty, entail a fine in the amount of fifty monthly calculation indicators.
